3. **Services** (`/services`)

     - Ship repair
     - Waste removal
     - Tank cleaning (HFO, fuel, water, sewage)
     - Discharging of sewage
     - Discharging of contaminated water
     - Disposal of solid waste
     - Disposal of liquid waste
     - Disposal of hazardous waste
     - Storage of fuel
     - Supply of sewage tanks
     - Supply of workers

4. **Projects** (`/projects`)
   - Gallery grid of completed work visuals
   - Category labels and professional captions

5. **References** (`/references`)
   - Client references in polished card layout

6. **Contact** (`/contact`)
   - Address and contact information
   - Contact form
   - Google map section

### Global Elements
- Sticky header with desktop and mobile navigation
- Persistent CTA button (“Request a Quote”)
- Strong informational footer with quick links and contacts

## 3) Revised Brand Direction

### Brand Positioning
A serious, operationally capable Namibian B2B services provider for marine environmental support and waste oil management.

### Tone of Voice
- Corporate and direct
- Trust-focused
- Safety- and compliance-oriented
- Operationally confident

### Visual Direction
- **Palette:** Deep navy, charcoal, white, muted teal accent
- **Typography:** Strong sans-serif hierarchy with high readability
- **Layout:** Spacious sections, clean grid, measured use of shadows
- **Components:** Professional cards, restrained accents, structured CTAs
- **Imagery:** Marine operations, industrial handling, tank and waste workflow visuals

### UX Direction
- Clear path to conversion on every page
- Mobile-first responsive behavior
- Sticky navigation and persistent contact access
- SEO-friendly section heading hierarchy
