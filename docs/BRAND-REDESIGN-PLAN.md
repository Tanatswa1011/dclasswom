## 3. Revised Brand Direction

**Brand voice**
- Corporate, direct, professional
- Confident but not boastful
- Technical enough to signal expertise; accessible for decision-makers

**Visual identity**
- **Primary palette:** Deep navy (#0F172A), Charcoal (#1E293B), White (#FFFFFF)
- **Accent:** Muted teal/green (#0D9488, #14B8A6)
- **Supporting:** Light grey (#F8FAFC, #E2E8F0)

**Typography**
- Headlines: Bold, authoritative sans (e.g. DM Sans, Satoshi, or similar)
- Body: Clean, readable sans
- Avoid decorative or playful fonts

**Imagery**
- Marine operations, ports, tank cleaning, industrial waste handling
- Real work environments — vessels, tanks, crews, facilities
- Environmental/conservation shots where relevant
- High-quality, professional stock or commissioned photos

**Principles**
- Premium industrial — serious, operational, established
- No flashy SaaS/startup vibes
- Trust through clarity and structure
- Space over clutter; cards, sections, clear hierarchy
